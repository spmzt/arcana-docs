# The seven Archives

Arcana's internals are named after the seven Archives of *Trinity
Seven*, each a domain of what an agent does: the agent's source is laid
out by them (`superbia/`, `invidia/`, `ira/`, `acedia/`, `avaritia/`,
`gula/`, `luxuria/`). This chapter is Arcana's own `docs/NAMING.md`,
included from its tree.
