# A tour of the panel

This part walks through the panel the way a new user meets it, a host
at a time, with a picture of each step. The pictures are the panel as
it is, taken by Arcana's own tests: of a fleet of three hosts made for
them (`hq`, `branch-a`, `branch-b`, numbered from the documentation
ranges), and, for what that fleet does not have, of the browser test's
own host, `lab`, with four jails (`cache`, `db`, `gw`, `web`).
